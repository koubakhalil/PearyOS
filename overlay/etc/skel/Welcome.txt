Welcome to PearyOS (live session)

 * You are logged in as "live" (no password). sudo works without a password.
 * This is a live system: changes are lost when you power off.
 * The pear button at the top left opens the app menu. The glass dock at the bottom is your
   taskbar: pinned apps plus every window that is open. The top bar has network, sound, power and the clock.
 * Ethernet: plug in a cable and it connects automatically (DHCP).
   Wi-Fi and VPN: click the network icon at the top right of the screen.
 * Glass effects: PearyOS picks the best mode for your machine automatically.
   Frosted blur needs OpenGL (a real GPU, or a VM that provides it).
   Without it you still get translucent glass, rounded windows and shadows, just no blur.
   Force a mode:   mkdir -p ~/.config/pearyos && echo full > ~/.config/pearyos/glass-mode   (full | soft | lite | off), then log out and in.
 * Problems with network or boot? Open a terminal and run:  pearyos-diag
 * Install more software:  sudo apt update && sudo apt install <package>

PearyOS is built from Ubuntu 24.04 (noble) packages with the Xfce desktop.
